# ASCEND Hero 3: Bauhaus + Unity

## Campaign

- **Archetype:** Hero
- **Design style:** Bauhaus (Modernist)
- **Persuasion principle:** Unity
- **Brand and product:** ASCEND premium plain white shirt
- **Audience:** Young people who value confidence, individuality, achievement, and versatile everyday clothing
- **Core offer:** A clean, versatile white shirt positioned as a foundation for confidently creating your own identity and pursuing your goals
- **Canonical hero artboard:** 1600 x 900 px (16:9); layout adapts for narrower screens

## Finished design

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser. Its group and plain-shirt illustrations are original inline vector artwork made for this concept; no third-party images are used.

## Rationale

1. **Hero cues:** “We rise together” and “Move forward together” use active, determined language. The ascending steps, common route, and people progressing side by side express courage, resilience, achievement, and progress as ongoing effort.
2. **Unity:** The composition shows several people at different points on one upward path, wearing the same plain white shirt while pursuing their own goals. Supporting copy centers mutual encouragement and a shared commitment, not sameness. The CTA invites people to move forward together; it does not claim that buying a shirt creates belonging. The page explicitly says the shirt is optional and that participation does not require a membership or purchase. The people are a conceptual illustration, not a claim about an existing organized community.
3. **Bauhaus features:** The repository's Bauhaus research describes purposeful form, geometry used to organize information, clear hierarchy, and concise sans-serif typography. The hero adapts these features through the aligned two-column layout, simplified geometric people and shirt silhouettes, functional ascending platforms that show shared progress, bold display type, and clear product callouts. Red, yellow, black, and white support contrast without treating a primary-color palette as a rule. No unsupported shirt material or construction claims are made.
4. **Key prompt:** “Create a responsive 1600 x 900 (16:9) ASCEND advertising hero for the premium plain white shirt. The audience is young people who value confidence, individuality, achievement, and versatile everyday clothing. Present the clean, versatile shirt as a foundation for creating a personal identity and pursuing goals. Combine the Hero archetype's courage, determination, resilience, achievement, and progress with Bauhaus geometry, purposeful composition, clear typographic hierarchy, and simplified forms. Use Unity as the persuasion mechanism: depict diverse people moving up a shared route and encouraging one another while pursuing their own goals. Make the shirt visibly important but optional; do not imply a real named group or that purchasing creates membership. Include a strong shared-action headline, brief copy, CTA, and original illustration. Do not use urgency or inventory messaging.”
