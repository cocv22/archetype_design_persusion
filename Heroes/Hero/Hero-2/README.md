# ASCEND Hero 2: Pop Art + Scarcity

## Campaign

- **Archetype:** Hero
- **Design style:** Pop Art (Postmodernist)
- **Persuasion principle:** Scarcity
- **Brand and product:** ASCEND premium plain white shirt
- **Audience:** Young people who value confidence, individuality, achievement, and versatile everyday clothing
- **Core offer:** A clean, versatile white shirt positioned as a foundation for confidently creating your own identity and pursuing your goals
- **Canonical hero artboard:** 1600 x 900 px (16:9); layout adapts for narrower screens

## Finished design

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser. The plain white-shirt artwork is an original inline vector illustration made for this concept. It does not reproduce a historical artwork, comic character, or third-party product photograph.

## Rationale

1. **Hero cues:** “Turn can't into did!” and “Make your mark” frame self-definition and goals as something pursued through persistence. The prominent white shirt, motion marks, and “NO QUIT!” callout reinforce confidence, resilience, and earned progress.
2. **Scarcity:** A visible callout presents a finite first edition as fictional campaign messaging. It invents no quantity, stock level, or deadline. Before publication, confirm a genuine production cap and accurate size availability; otherwise remove or revise the claim.
3. **Pop Art features:** The repository's Pop Art research describes popular commercial imagery, flat color, strong outlines, halftone patterns, comic speech text, and repeated product imagery. This design applies those features in an original, high-contrast white-shirt illustration with screen-print-like dots, comic-style “RISE!” and “NO QUIT!” bursts, and a strip of repeated shirt prints beneath one enlarged featured shirt. The bright flat palette and hard-edged print treatment make the product read like a mass-media campaign image rather than the geometric, restrained Bauhaus composition in Hero-1. No protected character or historical artwork is copied.
4. **Key prompt:** “Create a responsive 1600 x 900 (16:9) advertising landing-page hero for ASCEND's premium plain white shirt. Keep the campaign audience of young people who value confidence, individuality, achievement, and versatile everyday clothing, and the offer of a clean, versatile foundation for creating your own identity and pursuing your goals. Make the design clearly Pop Art: use flat commercial colors, a bold outlined original plain white-shirt illustration, halftone print texture, comic-style action text, and repeated small shirt images with one enlarged featured product. Express the Hero archetype through courage, persistence, confidence, resilience, and earned achievement. Make scarcity visible as fictional finite-first-edition campaign messaging; do not invent a quantity, actual stock, or deadline. State that any real production cap and shirt-size availability must be confirmed before publication. Include a strong headline, brief support copy, clear CTA, and responsive layout.”

## Research connection

The design adapts the features described in the repository's [Pop Art research](../../../design_styles/post-modernism_styles/pop_art.md), especially its observations about flat color, bold outlines, halftone dots, and comic text in Roy Lichtenstein's *Look Mickey* (1961), as well as its suggestion to repeat a product image and break the pattern with a featured offer, based on Andy Warhol's *Campbell's Soup Cans* (1962). These are documented design connections, not source images used in the page. The research links directly to [MoMA's Pop Art overview](https://www.moma.org/collection/terms/pop-art), the [National Gallery of Art's *Look Mickey* record](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/71479-look-mickey), and [MoMA's *Campbell's Soup Cans* record](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/79809). No external image or product specification is used.
