# ASCEND Hero 1: Bauhaus + Scarcity

## Campaign

- **Archetype:** Hero
- **Design style:** Bauhaus (Modernist)
- **Persuasion principle:** Scarcity
- **Brand and product:** ASCEND premium plain white shirt
- **Audience:** Young people who value confidence, individuality, achievement, and versatile everyday clothing
- **Core offer:** A clean, versatile white shirt as a foundation for confidently creating your own identity and pursuing your goals
- **Canonical hero artboard:** 1600 x 900 px (16:9); layout adapts for narrower screens

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser. Its plain white-shirt illustration is original inline vector artwork created for this concept; no third-party images or image credits are needed.

## Rationale

1. **Hero cues:** “Become your own next” and “Choose your next step” pair confident, action-oriented language with a forward-facing white shirt and aligned progress marks. They frame identity and achievement as something the audience shapes through their own choices and effort.
2. **Scarcity:** A visible first-edition message presents a finite release as fictional campaign messaging. The page invents no quantity, stock level, or deadline. Before publication, set a genuine production cap and show accurate size availability; otherwise remove or revise the claim.
3. **Bauhaus adaptation:** The repository's Bauhaus research describes purposeful form, geometry as an organizing structure, clear hierarchy, and concise sans-serif poster typography. The hero adapts these features through a strict two-column grid, a circular field and aligned progress marks that organize the plain-shirt illustration, a simplified high-contrast garment silhouette, and a bold, readable typographic hierarchy. Red, yellow, black, and white support contrast without treating primary colors as a required formula. The design makes no unsupported claims about fabric or construction.
4. **Key prompt:** “Create a professional, responsive advertising hero for ASCEND's premium plain white shirt, for young people who value confidence, individuality, achievement, and versatile everyday clothing. Offer a clean, versatile foundation for expressing an identity and pursuing personal goals. Use the Hero archetype: courage, determination, confidence, and achievement through effort. Apply documented Bauhaus principles with a functional geometric layout, clear hierarchy, bold sans-serif typography, and a simplified, prominent plain white-shirt illustration. Communicate a finite first edition as fictional campaign messaging; do not invent a quantity, stock level, or deadline, and state that any real production cap and size availability must be confirmed before publication. Include a strong headline, concise support copy, clear CTA, and a reusable 1600 x 900 (16:9) composition.”
