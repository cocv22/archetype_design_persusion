# The Everyman Archetype

[Home](README.md)

## Motivation and cues

- **Audience motivation:** People want to feel accepted and included without needing status or special expertise. They value familiarity, fairness, usefulness, and a sense that a brand understands everyday life.
- **Visual cues:** Realistic everyday settings, candid groups, accessible layouts, familiar objects, and people represented across ordinary ages and backgrounds.
- **Verbal cues:** Plain language, a friendly and direct voice, shared experiences, and practical benefits without pretension.
- **When to use it:** Use Everyman cues when a brand serves a broad community and wants to feel approachable and dependable. Avoid claiming to speak for everyone while showing only one narrow experience.
- **Real brand example — IKEA:** IKEA describes its vision as creating a better everyday life for the many people and offers home furnishings for everyday use. I interpret that broad, practical positioning as Everyman-like; this is my reading, not an official archetype label. [IKEA: About us](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/this-is-ikea/about-us/).

## Brief

- **Audience:** Roommates and first-time renters furnishing a shared apartment on a limited budget.
- **Product:** A starter set of practical furniture and home essentials.
- **Core offer:** Find useful, affordable pieces that help a shared home feel comfortable.
- **Action:** Build a starter-room list.
- **Styles:** Proposed for this draft: International Style (modernist) and Memphis Design (postmodernist). Confirm these against the team's style coverage before producing final artwork.
- **Principles:** Proposed for this draft: Liking and Unity. Confirm these against the team's persuasion coverage before producing final artwork.

## Hero 1: International Style / Liking

**Image:** [Final hero image to be added: `assets/heroes/everyman/hero-1.png`]

**Headline:** Make your place feel like yours.

**Supporting copy:** Find useful furniture and home basics for the first place you share.

**CTA and destination:** Build your starter-room list → room-planning page.

**Why it works:** The familiar moving-in moment supports a friendly, relatable voice. Liking comes from warm, recognizable details rather than forced jokes or flattery. Adapt the International Style's readable grid to make prices and room ideas easy to scan.

## Hero 2: Memphis Design / Liking

**Image:** [Final hero image to be added: `assets/heroes/everyman/hero-2.png`]

**Headline:** Good things happen when everyone gets a say.

**Supporting copy:** Mix practical pieces and small personal touches to make a shared room work for all of you.

**CTA and destination:** Explore shared-room ideas → roommate inspiration page.

**Why it works:** The message recognizes the negotiation of shared living. Use a light, playful Memphis-inspired pattern while keeping the offer and CTA clear; cite the specific feature adapted from a style source.

## Hero 3: International Style / Unity

**Image:** [Final hero image to be added: `assets/heroes/everyman/hero-3.png`]

**Headline:** Set up a home that works for all of you.

**Supporting copy:** Start with the shared essentials, then make room for everyone's routines.

**CTA and destination:** Plan your shared space → shared-home checklist.

**Why it works:** Unity connects the offer to the group identity of a household. Show different roommates contributing to the same space and adapt the International Style's orderly layout to clarify the shared checklist.

## Hero 4: Memphis Design / Unity

**Image:** [Final hero image to be added: `assets/heroes/everyman/hero-4.png`]

**Headline:** Different tastes. One place to call home.

**Supporting copy:** Bring everyone's ideas together with affordable pieces for the spaces you share.

**CTA and destination:** Make a room plan together → collaborative planning page.

**Why it works:** The headline celebrates belonging while acknowledging individual differences. A Memphis-inspired composition can show varied preferences in one shared room; keep the design welcoming and the CTA visible.
