# ASCEND: The Dress Code Is a Suggestion

## Campaign

- **Archetype:** Outlaw
- **Design style:** Postmodern Classicism
- **Persuasion principle:** Commitment and Consistency
- **Brand and product:** ASCEND premium plain white shirt
- **Target audience:** People drawn to autonomy, individuality, authenticity, and self-expression; the Outlaw research describes audiences who value freedom and resistance to convention.
- **Core offer:** A premium plain white shirt as a clean base for personal style, paired with an optional way to state a self-chosen style rule.

## Design rationale

Hero-2 takes a different visual route from Hero-1's Deconstructivist fractured poster. It places the shirt in an oversized classical arch, then disrupts the formal display with contemporary annotation and direct Outlaw copy. The garment remains whole, plain, and prominent. The interactive style statement is optional and never gates the product link.

## Outlaw representation

“The dress code is a suggestion” challenges unnecessary conventions and centers personal autonomy. The shirt is an unmarked canvas, not a uniform that grants membership or status. The tone is defiant without relying on aggression or unsupported claims.

## Postmodern Classicism representation

The arch and column-like frame borrow recognizable classical forms, but change their scale and function: instead of a portrait or monument, the arch displays an everyday white shirt. A contemporary red annotation and shifted composition make the classical reference feel playful and current. The original SVG does not reproduce a historical building or artwork.

## Commitment and Consistency representation

Visitors may optionally write a short personal style rule, then see it reflected back as a reminder. The statement is editable and clearable, stays on the current page, and is not sent or saved. It is not consent to purchase, subscribe, or receive marketing. A direct skip-to-product link remains available whether or not someone writes a rule.

## Key prompt

Create a responsive ASCEND advertising hero for a premium plain white shirt using the Outlaw archetype, Postmodern Classicism, and Commitment and Consistency. Make an original SVG illustration of a blank white tee displayed in a dramatically enlarged classical arch, then interrupt the formal display with contemporary annotation and rebellious copy. Include an optional, editable personal style statement that is not tied to purchasing; make clearing and skipping easy, and keep the product link available at all times. Do not invent product claims, endorsements, scarcity, or urgency.
