# Scarcity

[Home](../README.md)

## Mechanism

People may value an opportunity more when availability is limited or when it may end. A genuine limit can make an offer more salient, but it does not make the product better. The constraint and its reason should be clear.

## When and how to use it

Use scarcity only for a real limit, such as a small production run, seasonal product, fixed number of seats, or offer with a genuine end date. Show the product and the exact limit or deadline. Use clear, readable type and accurate stock information; do not rely on alarm colors or vague “act now” language.

## Example

**Headline:** First print run: 100 shirts.

**Supporting copy:** “This design is printed once in a run of 100. Current sizes and quantities are shown below. No restock is planned.” Use these statements only if they are true; clarify whether the artwork may appear on a later edition.

**CTA and destination:** “Shop the first run” → the product page with live size availability and the stated restock policy.

**Why the mechanism is present:** The customer can see a specific, verifiable constraint that makes this edition finite.

## Misuse to avoid

Do not invent stock shortages, restart countdowns, or call standard inventory a limited edition. Keep deadlines, location or size limits, and extension updates accurate. False urgency can lead to rushed decisions and damage trust.
