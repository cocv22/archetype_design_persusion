The fit check, properly explained

Three small steps. A lot less shrugging.

Use a shirt you already like as your reference. This little guide is yours whether or not you shop ASCEND.

  1. 01

    Pick your favorite

    Choose a tee whose fit feels right. Lay it flat on a table and smooth it without stretching.

  2. 02

    Measure across

    Measure straight across the chest from one pit seam to the other. You can also note shoulder seam to shoulder seam.

  3. 03

    Compare like with like

    When a garment chart is available, compare the same laid-flat measurements. Cuts and size charts vary, so check the listed method.

This is a general measuring tip, not a claim about ASCEND shirt measurements or fit. No purchase, signup, email, or measurements shared with us are needed.
