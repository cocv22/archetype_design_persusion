# The Architect Archetype

[Home](README.md)

## Motivation and cues

- **Audience motivation:** People want to turn a complex idea into a coherent, well-built system. They value foresight, structure, precision, and the ability to see how the parts fit together.
- **Visual cues:** Grids, plans, modular forms, measured spacing, structural lines, material studies, and views that reveal how something is assembled.
- **Verbal cues:** Clear, deliberate language about planning, foundations, systems, and purposeful improvement.
- **When to use it:** Use Architect cues when a brand helps customers design, organize, or build something complex. Keep the work understandable; complexity alone does not prove expertise.
- **Real brand example — Autodesk:** Autodesk's design and make software supports architecture, engineering, construction, and product design. I read its focus on digital tools for planning and making as Architect-like; this is my interpretation of the brand's work, not an official archetype label. [Autodesk: Design and Make](https://www.autodesk.com/).

## Brief

- **Audience:** Homeowners planning a substantial renovation who need to coordinate ideas, rooms, and decisions.
- **Product:** A digital renovation-planning workspace.
- **Core offer:** Organize room plans, materials, and project decisions in one visual plan.
- **Action:** Start a sample project.
- **Styles:** Proposed for this draft: Bauhaus (modernist) and Deconstructivism (postmodernist). Confirm these against the team's style coverage before producing final artwork.
- **Principles:** Proposed for this draft: Authority and Social Proof. Confirm these against the team's persuasion coverage before producing final artwork.
