A tiny style guide for making a plain tee feel like your choice, not somebody else's dress code. Take the ideas; leave the rest.

  1. 01

    Change the silhouette

    Try it tucked, untucked, or with one sleeve casually rolled. Keep the version that feels like you.

  2. 02

    Break one expectation

    Pick one detail you usually avoid and test it with what you already own. One switch can change the whole mood.

  3. 03

    Retire the approval check

    Ask whether you feel good in it, not whether it follows an invisible rule. That's the whole test.
